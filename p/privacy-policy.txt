Privacy Policy

Last updated: [12-10-2025]

This Privacy Policy describes how our application (“the App”) handles user data.
We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how the App works.

1. Overview

The App is a simple shopping-list tool designed to help users create and manage shopping lists on their device.
The App is fully offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

2. No Internet Connection

The App does not connect to the internet.
All features work entirely offline.

No data is uploaded to external servers.

No data is shared with third parties.

No analytics or tracking tools are used.

3. Data Storage

The shopping lists you create are stored locally on your device only.

The App does not access or collect your personal information.

You can delete your lists at any time from within the App.

We cannot access or restore your locally stored data.

4. Permissions

The App does not request or require any device permissions.
It does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, or any other device information.

5. Use by Children

The App is not intended for use by children.
Anyone can use the App, but it is designed for general audiences and not specifically for children under 13 (or under applicable local age requirements).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If changes are made, the updated version will be available with a new “Last updated” date.
